PROJECTS

C.A.R.E. – Smart Wireless IoT-Enabled Location Tracker

C.A.R.E. is a capstone research project focused on developing an affordable wearable IoT emergency alert system designed to improve personal safety, especially for elderly individuals and persons with disabilities. Using an ESP32-based device with GPS technology, it allows users to send emergency alerts with their real-time location and identification details through a simple button press. The system aims to provide a faster, more accessible, and reliable way to request assistance during emergency situations using the Arduino framework.

SDCPay – A Card Payment System: Improving Digital Transfers at SDCA

SDCPay is a card-based payment system developed as an academic research project to improve cashless transactions at Saint Dominic College of Asia (SDCA). It aims to reduce long queues and payment delays by allowing students and staff to use reloadable RFID cards for purchasing food and school supplies. The system includes card authentication, balance management, transaction processing, transaction history, and administrative monitoring. Developed using Flutter, Node.js, Express, and MongoDB, SDCPay demonstrates how technology can improve the efficiency, convenience, and security of campus payment processes.
